Introducing scVIVA-Tools & scVI-Tools MCP

July 8, 2026 · 5 min read
Ori Kronfeld

Introduction​

Alongside scvi-tools v1.5.0, we're shipping two new packages that round out the ecosystem in very different directions: scVIVA-Tools, which consolidates seven spatial transcriptomics models into a single toolkit, and scVI-Tools MCP, which puts scvi-tools knowledge directly in reach of LLM coding assistants like Claude.
